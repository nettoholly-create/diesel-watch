Pump prices from FuelWatch WA; terminal gate and weekly averages from the Australian Institute of Petroleum; national stock, days of cover and retail stock-outs from Fuel Plan; state reserve locations from the WA Government fuel security updates. Terminal gate price is GST inclusive and excludes cartage and distributor margin, so a delivered farm price sits above it; off-road use may attract a fuel tax credit at the rate the ATO publishes. Site counts cover the centres named in each region, not every site in it, and FuelWatch is a price notification scheme rather than an availability register — a site can list a price and still be dry.
